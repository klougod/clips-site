Brainstorm Clips

A self-hosted tool that turns long-form video into short, captioned clips and publishes them to YouTube channels we manage.

What this is

Brainstorm Clips is a privately operated, self-hosted application (built on the open-source SupoClip project). It runs entirely on infrastructure we control — there is no public sign-up and no third-party hosting involved.

The application connects to YouTube via Google's OAuth so it can read basic channel information and upload finished clips to the specific YouTube channels we have explicitly connected and manage.

What it does with YouTube access

  • Reads basic metadata for the connected channel, so we can confirm we're publishing to the right one.
  • Uploads finished video clips to that connected channel on our behalf.
  • Never accesses, modifies, or reads any YouTube data outside the channels we've explicitly connected.

See our Privacy Policy for full details.
