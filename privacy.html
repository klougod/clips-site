Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers Clips Site, a privately operated, self-hosted application that connects to YouTube via Google OAuth to publish short clips to YouTube channels we own and manage. It is not a public service — there is no third-party sign-up, and no one outside our organization can connect an account to it.

Who operates this

Operated by Bruno, contactable at brunoomagalhaes98@gmail.com for any question about this policy or the data described here.

What Google/YouTube data we access

When a YouTube channel is connected, the application requests:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload finished video clips to that channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read basic channel metadata (name, id), so the application can confirm it's publishing to the correct channel.

We only ever act on channels we have explicitly connected through this application's own account-connection flow. We do not access, read, or modify any YouTube data belonging to channels we have not connected.

What we do with this data

  • Upload video clips we have produced to the connected channel.
  • Display the connected channel's name/id back to us inside the application, so we know which account is linked.
  • Store OAuth access/refresh tokens, encrypted at rest, on our own self-hosted server — not with any third-party service.

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data retention

Connection tokens and associated metadata are retained for as long as the channel remains connected. Disconnecting a channel (in the application's settings, or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions) deletes the stored tokens.

Data sharing

Video files are uploaded directly to YouTube via Google's official API. No video content or channel data is shared with any other third party or service.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date above.
